ARTWise

A gallery companion that helps art enthusiasts discover the stories behind artists and artworks—and move confidently from exploration to purchase.

My roleUX Designer
Duration8 weeks
ContextUX certificate project
FocusDiscovery · Checkout

THE OPPORTUNITY

Let every artwork open a richer story.

Gallery visitors often want to know more than a wall label can tell them, yet existing digital experiences can make artist information difficult to find and purchasing unnecessarily complicated.

ARTWise brings artist biographies, artwork details and checkout into one approachable mobile experience—supporting deeper engagement without interrupting the gallery visit.

03 · TEST & REFINE

Small changes made exploration feel more direct.

Two rounds of usability testing exposed where people lost momentum and guided focused improvements to discovery, artwork details and checkout.

ROUND 01

Make artist information easier to reach.

Participants struggled to access artist profiles and expected artwork details directly from the home screen.

ROUND 02

Bring useful paths into the open.

An Artists section, “See all” links and a clear “Read more” action shortened the route to deeper information.

BEFORE → AFTER

Manage quantity without leaving the cart.

Simple plus and minus controls let shoppers adjust an artwork order in place, reducing uncertainty and unnecessary steps.

PURCHASE JOURNEY

Make each checkout step predictable.

A structured flow connects the cart, delivery details, payment and confirmation so users always understand what comes next.

HIGH-FIDELITY PROTOTYPE

Art discovery with a clear path forward.

The final flow combines artwork browsing, rich artist context and a streamlined checkout in one calm, gallery-inspired interface.

OUTCOME

A gallery companion designed for curiosity and confidence.

“ARTWise has made exploring artworks and connecting with artists easier than ever—an exciting addition to the art world.”
