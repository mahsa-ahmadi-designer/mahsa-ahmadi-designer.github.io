Engaging Explorations

A participatory design project with Östergötlands Museum, creating an Activity Booklet to enhance visitor engagement—especially for families with children.

My roleProject Coordinator · Visual Designer
Duration8 weeks
Target audienceFamilies with children
ContextParticipatory Design course

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Creating a more engaging museum experience.

The product

Our project resulted in the creation of an engaging Activity Booklet designed to enhance the museum experience for visitors. The booklet features a short introduction to the museum, gamification activities like a trivia game and scavenger hunt, an event calendar, and a tear-off feedback sheet for visitors. The back cover includes a convenient museum map, aiding navigation.

The problem

Östergötlands Museum aimed to enhance visitor engagement, especially with families. While the museum already featured interactive elements, there was a need for further innovation to captivate younger audiences.

The goal

The goal of this project was to explore innovative ways to enhance engagement and interaction between Östergötlands Museum and its visitors, particularly families with children. Through participatory design and user research, the aim was to identify and implement a creative solution that fosters meaningful connections, facilitates exploration, and enriches the overall museum experience.

Course information

This project was completed as part of the Participatory Design course at Linköping University during the third semester in 2023.

PROJECT JOURNEY · 01

Ideation

Process overview

  • Conducted meetings with the museum's contact person.
  • Brainstormed ideas based on the museum's engagement needs and user information.

Concept presentation

  • Provided the stakeholder with three concepts, each with detailed information about purpose, functionality, target audience, and requirements.
  • Stakeholder selected the idea of creating a booklet.

PROJECT JOURNEY · 02

Design

Feedback collection

  • Designed a flyer with a call to action to gather visitor feedback on preferred activities.
  • Questions included preferences for activities like trivia games, scavenger hunts, labyrinths, exhibition checklists, and more.

Prototype design

  • Developed a lo-fi prototype based on visitor feedback.
  • Conducted a workshop with the target audience to test and evaluate the product.
  • Revised and designed a hi-fi prototype based on workshop feedback.

Low-fidelity prototype

Usability study: workshop findings

  • Text readability issues on dark backgrounds were identified.
  • Concerns about the QR code potentially contributing to cheating in the Trivia game.
  • Printing issues affected the legibility of the body copy.
  • Suggestions included adding more activities and expanding to the art section.

PROJECT JOURNEY · 03

Design refinement

  • Background Color Adjustment: Improved contrast for better readability.
  • Map Font & Size Optimization: Optimized for clarity and easy navigation.
  • Trivia Hunt Differentiation: Introduced distinct boxes for enhanced visibility.
  • Improved Margins Post Proof Print: Adjusted for optimal printing quality.
  • Addition of Museum Logo: Reinforced branding with the museum logo.
  • New QR Code Method: Implemented to prevent potential cheating.
  • Enhanced Line Height: Improved overall readability.

GOING FORWARD

Impact, learning, and next steps.

IMPACT · TAKEAWAYS

  • Utilized a gamification approach that engaged families with children.
  • Families could complete activities together, enhancing the museum experience.
  • Facilitated an enjoyable way for children to learn about history.
  • Stakeholder expressed satisfaction with the project outcome.

WHAT I LEARNED

  • Recognized the crucial role of user feedback and user-centered design.
  • Appreciated the value of diverse expertise in participatory design.

Next steps

01

Swedish Version Implementation

Given the time constraints during the project, creating a Swedish version of the booklet was not feasible. The next step involves implementing a Swedish version to cater to a wider audience, addressing the language barrier for visitors.

02

Expansion of Activities

Insights from the workshop highlighted a desire for more activities in the booklet. The limited timeframe prevented the inclusion of additional activities in the hi-fi prototype. Moving forward, the plan is to enrich the booklet by adding more engaging activities, enhancing the overall visitor experience.
