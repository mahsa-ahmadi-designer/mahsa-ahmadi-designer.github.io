THE OPPORTUNITY

Match people—not only rooms.

Finding a shared home is only one part of the challenge. Personality differences, routines, cleanliness standards and privacy concerns can turn a practical match into a difficult living experience.

HarmonyHome explores a more human approach: detailed profiles and compatibility signals help people find roommates and spaces that better align with how they actually live and communicate.

01 · UNDERSTANDING THE USER

What makes shared living feel compatible?

Semi-structured interviews with young adults revealed that compatibility is shaped by far more than location and price. Personality, lifestyle, cleanliness and daily routines all affect whether a shared home works.

04 · TEST & REFINE

Feedback made the matching experience more direct.

Participants valued the idea of using social connections to improve compatibility, but unclear labels and navigation caused confusion. Three focused changes improved clarity and matching accuracy.

Merge competing actions

“Save to the App” and “Save to Profile” became one clear Save button, reducing hesitation and simplifying the flow.

Surface relevant content sooner

The home page became dynamic, using preferences chosen during account creation to shorten the path to relevant rooms and roommates.

Connect more than one network

Expanded social-network integration created richer compatibility signals and supported a more accurate matching experience.

OUTCOME

A more human model for roommate matching.

HarmonyHome demonstrates how user-centered design and insights from psychology and sociology can make compatibility visible, reduce conflict and improve the experience of finding a shared home.

Interdisciplinary thinking helped connect design decisions with real relationship dynamics.

Iterative feedback turned a promising concept into a clearer, more usable matching experience.
