Work

A selection of professional and academic product-design work, from early research to tested interfaces.

01 · PROFESSIONAL · INVOLING AB · 2025

WanderHon

AI-driven travel discovery, designed from research through developer handoff.

Over four months as a UX/UI Design Intern, followed by consulting work, I shaped the mobile experience from competitive research and journey mapping to flows, high-fidelity prototypes and a reusable design system. Two rounds of usability testing led to changes including swipe navigation, draggable map overlays, a shorter onboarding path, clearer save feedback and more scannable content.

Product design Mobile Prototyping Usability testing Design system
02 · MASTER THESIS · 2024

HarmonyHome

A roommate-matching concept built around personality, lifestyle and communication styles. I used interviews, competitive research, personas, journey mapping and user flows to shape the experience, then tested the interface and refined saving, navigation, personalized content and social features. The concept also included language selection and a high-contrast mode.

UX research Product design Usability testing Accessibility
03 · GOOGLE UX CERTIFICATE · 2023—2024

ARTWise

A digital gallery experience designed to connect art enthusiasts with artists and artworks. The project explored how clearer discovery and purchasing journeys could make browsing art more approachable, moving from UX research and structure into wireframes and interactive prototypes.

UX/UI User flows Wireframing Prototyping
04 · CO-DESIGN · ÖSTERGÖTLANDS MUSEUM

Engaging Explorations

A participatory design project exploring how a museum visit could become more engaging for younger visitors. Working with Östergötlands Museum, we used co-design and playful, gamified activities to investigate new ways of encouraging exploration and interaction with the museum experience.

Co-design Participatory design Service design Gamification
05 · AUGMENTED REALITY · TEAM PROJECT

OH DEER!

An immersive AR concept exploring how everyday habits affect the environment. As a team, we designed a dynamic experience in which users’ choices visibly change the landscape, combining interaction, emotional narratives and personalized feedback to encourage reflection on more sustainable behavior.

Augmented reality Interaction design Concept design Sustainability
