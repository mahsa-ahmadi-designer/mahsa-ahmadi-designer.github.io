HOW I WORK

I use prototypes as a thinking tool — to make ideas tangible, expose uncertainty and learn before development gets expensive.

ABOUT

Research-minded.
Interaction-focused.
Detail-aware.

I’m especially drawn to prototyping because it connects strategy with reality. I like taking something abstract — a user need, a complicated flow or an early idea — and shaping it into something that can be experienced, questioned and improved.

My work spans research, information architecture, interaction and visual design. Accessibility is built into that process rather than added at the end.
