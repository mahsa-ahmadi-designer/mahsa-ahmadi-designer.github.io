ABOUT

Designing with curiosity,
testing with purpose.

I’m especially drawn to prototyping: it’s where an idea becomes tangible enough to question, test and improve. My process combines user-centered thinking with detailed interaction and visual design.

Accessibility is part of how I design, not a final check. I’ve worked with WCAG AAA contrast, high-contrast modes and language accessibility, and I build systems that make consistency easier to maintain.
