Letters as structure

A letter already has a shape people recognise before they read it. That is the part I use.

A name has to flow, hold together, and still read when it is small.

These began with Armenian letterforms and names, and grew into rings, bracelets and pendants. The work is all in the balance — a name has to flow, hold together as an object, and still be readable once it is small enough to wear.

Some of these were made with Farhadian Gold, including the gold and leather cuff and the pavé-set name pendants.
