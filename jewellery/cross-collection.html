Cross Collection

A family of cross pendants developed from hand-rendered studies through casting and stone-set final pieces for Farhadian Gold.

Farhadian GoldDesigned for
Concept to productionProcess
Precious metal, pavé settingMaterials

One symbol, five distinct characters

The cross is immediately recognisable. The design work lies in changing its character without losing the clarity of the symbol.

Each pendant began as a graphite study. I used proportion, negative space, polished surfaces and stone placement to move between architectural, ornamental and softer organic expressions.

The sketches solve the structure; the finished pieces reveal how light completes it.

The collection was developed for Farhadian Gold. My work covered the design and final model, while casting, finishing and stone setting were completed through the workshop.
