About

Industrial design training, worked through in metal.

I studied industrial design and spent years designing jewellery, watches and accessories — from first concept through to the final model. Later I added user-centred design, which changed how I research and test an idea before committing to it.

I am drawn to work that blends cultural heritage with contemporary form. A piece can carry a myth, a queen, a letter of an alphabet, and still sit quietly on a hand.

I design through to hand-off and final model. The casting and production are done by the workshops and houses I design for.
