Two houses, one design language

This collection brings together pieces I created for two jewelry houses: Farhadian Gold in 2018 and Dafineh Gallery in 2019, with a small number of designs released in 2020.

Nature is the starting point; simplicity gives each piece its character.

Flowers, leaves and minimalist art shaped the rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets. Across the collection, I focused on modern, wearable forms with elegant proportions and refined details.

Although the pieces were created for different companies and in different years, they share a coherent visual language: clean lines, organic references and carefully considered details.

I developed each design through to the final model, while casting and production were completed by the respective workshops.
