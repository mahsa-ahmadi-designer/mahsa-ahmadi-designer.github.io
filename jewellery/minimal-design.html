Taking things away

These pieces were designed for Dafineh Gallery and Farhadian Gold. The brief was modern and wearable, so I started from organic shapes — flowers, leaves and hearts — and kept removing until only the line was left.

Take away everything the shape does not need, then stop.

I draw on a face or a hand outline early. A shape can look right on paper and then look wrong next to a jaw, or between two knuckles, and it is cheaper to find that out with a pencil.

The collection includes eternity and Afra, each in three variations, a spiral, a group set with coloured stones, and one in mother of pearl.

Casting and production were completed through the respective workshops. My work continued through the final model.

Dafineh Gallery

The Dafineh collection was designed in 2019, with selected pieces released in 2020. Flowers, leaves and minimalist art become clean, modern rings, necklaces and earrings.

Afra

Afra translates leaf forms into a light geometric family. Marquise stones become small leaves, while the open gold structure keeps the pendant and earrings delicate.

03

Spiral

A continuous line becomes the focal point of a minimal ring, turning a familiar natural rhythm into a compact, wearable form.

04

Coloured Stones

Green, purple and clear stones bring color into the collection without changing its minimal language. The settings remain light so the stones define the rhythm.

05

Mother of Pearl

The soft sheen of mother of pearl replaces a conventional gemstone, creating a calm focal surface within the same reduced geometric language.
