Think Out Of The Box

Concept and experimental work, mostly in stainless steel. Closer to industrial design than to traditional jewelry.

2019—2021Years

Stainless steel, enamelMaterials

Ideas that would not survive a brief

This is where I test the ideas that would not survive a client brief. Science fiction, technology, film.

Mystery came out of Kubrick's 2001. The Drone Ring and Sci-Fly came from wanting jewelry that looks like equipment rather than ornament. Neon is stainless steel with phosphor-green enamel.

Steel does not forgive a soft curve. That turned out to be useful.

Working in steel changed how I draw. It does not forgive a soft curve the way gold does, so the sketches got more technical and I started making properly dimensioned drawings.

As a consultant for Vitaly in Toronto I designed contemporary pieces in stainless steel, including Proxy, Reset and Spark. Proxy and Spark are still on sale today, both carrying a bestseller badge on the brand's own store.
